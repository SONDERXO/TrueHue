Mark the facial skin we should correct

Three reference images. Draw your own boundaries; no model mask is prefilled. This is a small development guide, not final thesis ground truth.

  1. Choose Facial skin. Click around the visible face, then Finish polygon. Multiple skin polygons are allowed.
  2. Choose Keep excluded. Draw separate polygons over eyes, eyebrows, lips/mouth and any hair, paint, clothing or background inside your outline. Include the missed cheek if it is actual skin.
  3. Use Uncertain where the boundary is unclear. Keep a little distance from a blurry edge if you cannot tell.
  4. Check your outlines, mark the image complete, then move to the next image. Download the JSON when finished.
What about the neck and progress saving?

This set focuses on facial coverage. Leave the person's neck unmarked for now; unmarked pixels are unknown, not rejected skin. The face-and-neck requirement remains. Exclusions take priority over uncertain regions, which take priority over skin. These labels do not change ITA measurements or training data automatically.

Only completed polygons are saved locally. Unfinished polygon points are not saved. Browser storage may be unavailable; download a draft before closing. You can import the JSON to continue.

Click around the outline in order. Click the first point again to save, or use Finish / save polygon. Crossing edges must be undone before saving.
