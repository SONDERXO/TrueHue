# Optional correction-capture feasibility pilot

11 November2026 | Resource assessment draft; no collection or training selected

**Question:** can a device produce usable same-scene exposure/white-balance pairs
with an acceptable original reference, without confusing camera processing and
motion with the intended correction? This tests data feasibility, not model
accuracy, physical skin colour or a finished training dataset. It is a separately
declared extension to the paper's original-photo synthetic restoration study.

First establish device/app model and whether exposure and white balance can be
locked independently. No hardware purchase, reference chart, physical skin-tone
measurement or new MST label is assumed. Device specifications must be checked
before giving model-specific capture instructions. No photos are needed now.

If feasible and selected later, bound the initial pilot to one voluntary adult,
one session/scene and at most six photographs: a chosen reference, its repeat,
one darker exposure, one brighter exposure, one cooler and one warmer white-
balance setting. All attempts, metadata and failures stay in the record; no
replacement until a separately reviewed reason. Do not contact anyone or assume
access/permission. A single person/device supplies feasibility evidence only.

Hold scene, framing, pose and focus as stable as the device permits; record actual
settings and unsupported locks. No unverified EV-to-gamma or temperature-to-RGB-
gain formula is permitted. A changed camera setting does not provide an exact
inverse-control label. The user must choose the reference appearance separately
from masks. Reference repeats are different captures, not exact identity controls;
exact no-change controls would be copies of the chosen reference.

Before any collection, write the selected session's plan/limits, storage/group
roles and intended file access. No existing final/locked data change roles. Before
any new processing, audit actual file membership, known source grouping and
exposure/colour outcomes. Do not create a final test from this tiny pilot or
claim verified person independence from filenames.

Real captures may move, change shadows or undergo nonlinear camera processing.
Assess correspondence before paired-pixel scores; do not assume registration,
compare unmatched skin regions, invent approximate targets without checking the
actual renderer, or automatically add a registration model. Fit/render targets,
if later selected, are reference-informed supervision and must record residuals;
they are never automatic correction predictions or inference inputs.

Technical failures, unavailable supports, clipping and undesirable reference
appearance remain explicit. No threshold/crop/mask rescues or automatic annotation
queue follows a failed pilot. Stop if the device cannot produce controlled pairs
or if correspondence/three-control representability does not support the intended
test. Do not enlarge the pilot to force success or make it an obligatory project.

Only after actual feasibility evidence would a separate data/training comparison
be specified. The final predictor remains one decision tree with ITA/MST and
gamma/red/blue outputs; app input remains one photograph. This draft authorizes
no acquisition, photography, recruitment, upload, inference, fitting or runtime
change. The research team owns the decision; adviser action is not required.
