**MM-CelebA-HQ:**30,000 CelebA-HQ face images with semantic masks, sketches,
descriptions and background-removed versions. Semantic masks come from manually
annotated CelebAMask-HQ. This offers potential measurement/support evaluation
annotations, not calibrated skin-colour reference targets. No documented MST
labels, measured colour or source-to-corrected-colour pairs established from the
repository. Rendered3DMM images, generated edits, sketches and transparent copies
are not corrected-colour truth. Raw/annotation links were removed; the repository
directs users to the original source. Identity information needs the CelebA team;
MM/CelebA-HQ, CelebAMask-HQ and STW's CelebA records may share sources/people.
Noncommercial research/reproduction restrictions apply through the source terms.

**FFHQ:**70,000 aligned/cropped1024x1024 Flickr face PNGs, per-image source/author/
licence metadata and original-image links. It is a possible additional source for
synthetic restoration of the supplied photographic appearance or difficult-case
mask checks, after quality/support and grouping admission. It is not documented
as an instrument-calibrated true-colour dataset, expert-corrected pair set or MST
label collection. High resolution is not evidence of correct colour. Alignment
and cropping can limit neck coverage; repeated people or multiple crops from the
same source require grouping, not reliance on the publisher image split. Dataset
metadata/docs are CC BY-NC-SA4.0; individual photo licences vary and carry their
own attribution requirements. Dataset forbids face-recognition development: do
not introduce an identity-recognition training task to solve grouping.

## Recommendation

Keep STW for possible label evidence. Treat MM-CelebA-HQ/CelebAMask-HQ primarily
as an annotation/support-evaluation lead, and FFHQ as a possible photographic
source pool. Neither solves measured correction references merely by being sharp
or large. Do not start another small image review, fit or replace admitted data
automatically. Prefer resolving current MST evidence and existing PPR reference/
support blockers before adding a redundant photographic pool.

These are research leads only, not adopted datasets or an amended thesis. Before
any actual method/dataset activation, record its scope and evidence in the panel
register, keeping final roles and exposure locks. Current experimental four-
control smooth candidate remains frozen; runtime still has three outputs.
