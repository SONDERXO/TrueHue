# Alternatives after the continuing PIQ23 website error

17 November 2026, Asia/Manila. User reports the PIQ23 website still produces an error and asks for alternatives. This does not verify the cause, inbox state or failure of every access route. No duplicate contact was sent.

Checked the current handoff, previous alternatives/source-review records and cached proposal PDF pages 63 and 65. The written method learns synthetic-to-original correction parameters; PIQ23 is optional. Replacing its facial-exposure supervision with another corpus would be a separate research extension. This documentation comparison does not select a new source, model or fit, amend the method, or reopen closed studies.

## Bounded comparison

- GFIQA-20k: the official University of Konstanz page describes 20,000 human-face images with subjective quality ratings and research-community availability. It advertises images, JPEG versions and MOS/individual ratings. The inspected page does not establish exposure-specific preference labels, signed EV or corrected targets. Both official archive links redirected the web reader to Microsoft sign-in; successful download was not established. This is a closer content match than whole-image IQA, but not a verified replacement for PIQ23 exposure ranking. No third-party mirror was acquired or treated as permission.
- SPAQ: the official README again lists 11,125 smartphone pictures with overall quality, brightness, colourfulness, contrast, noisiness, sharpness and scene labels including human. This repeats the 16 November documentation comparison, not a new discovery. These are whole-image annotations; brightness is not facial acceptable-exposure preference. The README-linked Google Drive folder returned 404 through the reader. Baidu availability and recipient terms were not verified. No pretrained model was selected.
- ReLI-QA: the official repository advertises image access through Baidu and public MOS metadata. Its public CSV header includes luminous intensity, global naturalness and local naturalness. The authors' IEEE abstract reports 840 relighted outputs from six selected head images. These are relighting-quality examples, with limited source diversity; they are not established corrected photographic exposure targets or PIQ23-equivalent labels. Repository MIT licensing alone does not establish all image rights. No image archive was accessed.

Conclusion: alternatives exist as research resources, but this bounded check established no immediately usable equivalent to the approved PIQ23 facial-exposure supervision. Do not promise a dataset swap repairs the model or finishes the Friday work. The paper permits a route without PIQ23, but the current record contains no distinct supported independent next correction fit. A new candidate must supply a specific additional learning rationale and prospective assessment, rather than repeat the failed 11-source studies or generic quality-model search.
