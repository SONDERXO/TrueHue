## Fixed evaluation and acceptance

Evaluate all756old principal attempts:420old development conditions via their
held-key model,336already-exposed later conditions via the full fit. Reuse their
saved actual-input masks, alpha, scoring supports, features and source grouping.
Thus the comparison changes training sources rather than evaluation regions.
No evaluation image or label enters fitting; any new-row features/calibration
remain independent of the held key. Later images are previously exposed checks,
not a fresh final test.

For each cohort separately, require:

1. Zero changed usable clean photos:22development and18later. Report14clean
   measurement failures separately; they are not learned preservation.
2. No increase in core or stress mean regional RGBMAE **or CIEDE2000**, compared
   with the aligned parent on the exact same supports/conditions.
3. No increase in core or stress harmful-condition counts under either RGBerror
   or CIEDE2000; report new harm cases separately from lost old harms.
4. No decrease in core or stress beneficial-condition counts under either metric.
   This prevents passing merely by disabling useful corrections.
5. No outside-support or protected-pixel changes. Reproduce exported-tree
   decisions, costs, clean constraints, exclusions and rendered outputs.

Report worst harm, per-publisher means, availability and known clean regression
cases alongside averages. No rounding of source decimal scores into integer
MST bins or fairness pass. Inspect all fixed new-input mask boards and all fixed
evaluation correction boards, plus explicitly outcome-selected worst cases.

If any requirement fails, retain the rejected candidate and stop this comparison;
no threshold, weight, feature, crop or depth retuning on these results. Do not
open the new holdout to search for a favorable conclusion.

## Closed holdout and study scope

The8reserved groups/24photos remain byte-unread throughout preparation and
model repair. Only after the fixed development comparison passes and the complete
model/pipeline/evaluation manifest is frozen should a separately recorded
confirmation phase process them once. Keep every holdout failure and stop tuning
against it. A later fresh final test still needs a defensible role definition.

No fresh[9,10]publisher group exists in this candidate pool. This cannot establish
independent all-MST fairness, the proposal's70/15/15split, verified-person
separation or real camera-bias correction. The masks remain imperfect. More
sources are a hypothesis about generalization, not a guaranteed solution.
