## Predictor and fixed leaf information

One hard binary tree, maximum depth4/31nodes, one reached leaf and exactly three positive controls gamma/red/blue. Keep the existing281feature interface/order available for branch tests; use ONLY two named existing coordinates inside every affine leaf: scene_log_r_over_g and scene_log_b_over_g (indices5and6 in the frozen interface). These are full-scene log((channel_mean+1)/(green_mean+1)), not per-pixel mean log-chroma or a newly inferred neutral reference. The +1 means gain translation is approximate, and clipping/gamma/material content can change the relationship. No pixels or canonical descriptors are recomputed at this stage.

Why these two: channel gains primarily change their corresponding red/green or blue/green relationship, giving two input directions a leaf could use independently instead of averaging all colour targets. This is a predeclared mechanistic choice, not a subset selected from errors, target correlations or a grid. It adds no new image information and may fail because source complexion, illumination and backgrounds also change these coordinates. The remaining features, including inputITA/MST, remain available to the single tree's branches; they do not define a brightening rule. No all281-coordinate leaf regression.

Every leaf stores three affine rows over these two coordinates and an intercept:9float64 coefficients, versus846for all281coordinates. A full16leaf tree therefore stores144leaf coefficients plus at most45axis-branch scalars,189numeric scalars before metadata. This is a static capacity count, not a trained model/phone benchmark. Three separate output formulas are part of ONE reached leaf, not three trees.

Generated branch tests are supplied fixtures, never optimized: a three-leaf depth2 tree branches on input_green_p50_codes, index13, with under at<=80, colour at>80and<=180, over at>180. Fixtures place under at64, colour at128 and over at224. Constant-only and one-leaf fixtures are also supplied. These arbitrary engineering values are NOT learned exposure thresholds or a selected real-data topology. No production correction tree is loaded, modified or used as initialization.

## One deterministic regularized leaf solve

For each populated leaf compute training-only means mu and population standard deviations s of its two float32 input coordinates. When s==0 exactly, use1 as the arithmetic scale; this gives a zero centered column, not a missing-feature fallback. Standardization is only for the two leaf inputs, never target normalization. With z=(x-mu)/s solve:

mean_n ||y_n - (b + A*z_n)||_2^2 + 0.01*||A||_F^2.

Lambda0.01 is a fixed engineering setting, not validated or tuned. Intercepts are unpenalized, raw three controls are targets, and every fitting row has equal weight. Compute centered sufficient statistics once, solve the same positive-definite2x2matrix for the three outputs, and fold mu/s into raw-input coefficients before serialization. Use one direct solve, no optimizer iterations, restarts, package selection, role weights, target scaling, coefficient pruning or alternative penalties. An independent scalar2x2inverse/sufficient-statistic reconstruction must agree within absolute/relative1e-12. Training residual reduction alone is not a quality claim because ridge trades residual against penalty.

Single-row and constant-target leaves are permitted engineering cases and must reduce to finite constant predictions where appropriate. Empty leaves are refused by the generated trainer; they are never silently assigned identity or a previous target. Nonfinite/wrong-width inputs and nonpositive/nonfinite targets are errors. Validate all generated fitting predictions before declaring a fit valid; a failed fit is preserved without retry.

2. **Neutral/constant outputs:** same nine inputs with all targets[1,1,1]; expect zero slopes and exact neutral predictions within arithmetic tolerance. 3. **Degenerate inputs:** one row; repeated identical coordinates with conflicting positive targets; a zero-variance coordinate; perfectly correlated coordinates; and an empty leaf. Require deterministic finite ridge solutions where defined and explicit empty-leaf rejection. Conflicting identical inputs retain irreducible error, not false separation.
4. **Extrapolation:** score x5=10,x6=0 with the separable toy leaf, which should produce a nonpositive red control and be rejected. Score x5=-10,x6=0 to expose a large positive extrapolated gain; report it without treating numerical validity as desirable correction. These are fixed probes, not deployed cutoffs.

No generated success threshold is imposed on this confounded set and no favorable-offset selection is allowed. Record whether the simple coordinates mistake source colour for an edit. This limitation is not a universal impossibility proof or a prerequisite for a perfect neutral oracle.
