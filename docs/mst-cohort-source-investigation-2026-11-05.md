## Recommended new lead: Stellenbosch controlled-lighting portraits

[Mbatha, Booysen and Theart, Skin Tone Estimation under Diverse Lighting Conditions](https://www.mdpi.com/2313-433x/10/5/109)
reports285participants/21,375images,15lighting conditions and5head rotations per
person. It describes manual MST labelling involving participant, assistant and
researcher, with a lookup chart. Extreme-tone representation is lower. The
[data statement](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11122461/) says data are
available on request. The
[author repository](https://github.com/skmbatha/MEng-Skin-tone-estimation-using-VGG-16)
describes scripts/models; it is not verified image/label access. The article names
Rensu P. Theart as corresponding author, `rptheart@sun.ac.za`.

**Research judgment:** this is the strongest new inquiry lead in this search for
matching photographic ITA measurements and independently supplied MST labels.
It is not a verified qualified cohort or a guarantee that ITA predicts MST.
We have not received the data, participant/label manifest, recipient terms or
category counts needed to design a calibration evaluation. No source code or
pretrained CNN was executed/adopted. The retained mapping/tree design remains.

An [unsent access request](mst-stellenbosch-access-request-draft-2026-11-05.md)
is prepared. It asks about noncommercial research/calibration use, exact MST
provenance, anonymous subject IDs, lighting/pose records, per-category subject
support and a bounded inspection sample before any bulk processing. This is a
concrete route to new evidence, not another old-subject readiness audit.
