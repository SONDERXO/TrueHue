# Focused correction-family method selection

15 November 2026. Decision: **NO SUPPORTED NEW TRAINING RECIPE SELECTED.** This bounded pass is complete; it does not establish that single-photo correction is impossible. The requested working model remains unfinished, with paper editing paused and November25 retained.

The goal remains useful automatic exposure AND colour correction from a single photograph, preserving acceptable photographed appearance and eventually demonstrating reduced bias against darker-skinned people. One traditional final tree, ITA/MST, face/visible-neck support and optional manual refinement remain the retained method. The five-control operator is an experimental extension, not adopted paper/runtime scope.

## What the saved evidence requires

The real five-control test improved four exposure-family means but failed colour restoration. Its saved-route diagnosis found three held colour inputs and one identity input entering pure-exposure leaves, despite zero mixed neutral/exposure fitting-leaf occurrences out of220. This supports a transfer problem in correction-family decisions; additional operator capacity alone has not repaired it. It does not prove every feature set or learning strategy fails.

Any new proposal must explain how information available in the distorted input helps distinguish an added error from acceptable natural appearance. A paired clean-reference feature difference is unavailable at inference. A held-error-specific guard, stronger output multiplier or another capacity/loss sweep has no demonstrated new information basis. No such intervention is selected here.

## Closed-history cross-check

Read the saved gray-pixel selector, highlight-context generated assessment, three-source sclera feasibility inspection, source-shape generated assessment, existing illumination-cue readiness and Re-IQA feasibility result. Gray selection did not establish a trusted cast measurement; encoded highlight intercepts had material/encoding ambiguity; sclera extraction was unavailable or anatomically unvalidated; gain-invariant shape also removes global acceptable-source offsets. Generic scene colour/brightness is confounded by content. These are limitations of the fixed investigated candidates, not universal impossibility certificates. Re-IQA is an explicit neural representation amendment with unresolved transfer/engineering evidence, not an available lightweight repair.

Also checked the saved-route diagnosis and the prior bagged-tree proposal. No closed gray/highlight/sclera/prior/ensemble/bright-scene branch is restarted, and no unused-feature screen, alternative mask, depth/weight/threshold grid or new review batch is selected.

## Practical consequence and next decision

Do not spend another fit/review cycle on the existing recipe. At present we lack an evidence-backed new inference cue AND an admitted independent tone-labelled correction cohort. The second gap prevents darker-skin qualification even if numeric development performance improves; FFHQ must not acquire inferred official MST labels.

A future continuation must introduce substantive new evidence or an explicit method-scope change. Under the retained traditional single-photo scope, a credible paired portrait corpus connecting content to acceptable reference appearance, with valid darker-tone evidence, is the concrete missing basis for a new learned prior. That is a prerequisite assessment, not authorization to restart the closed source hunt, acquire data or assume more data automatically fixes transfer. A neural representation proposal would instead require an explicit scope decision and its own preservation/transfer rationale; this pass does not recommend or launch it. There is no automatic next experimental queue.

Do not substitute an exposure-only/manual-only product or limitations-only thesis for the user's working-model goal. No claim of completion, fairness or photographic usefulness is made. No new human ratings are needed for this decision.
