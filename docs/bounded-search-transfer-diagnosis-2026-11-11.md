## Decision: investigate additional correction evidence before another fit

Keep the working-model objective and one decision tree with ITA/MST, local
single-photo inference, face/visible-neck support and optional manual refinement.
Do not declare the thesis a negative-results study or require adviser approval.
No more existing mask reviews are requested. The next evidence should test
correction need across source/content differences, not just lower training loss.

One OPTIONAL avenue is a tiny controlled-capture feasibility pilot: the same
scene photographed under recorded exposure/white-balance settings, with a chosen
photographic reference. It adds camera-rendered paired evidence rather than
another transform of the same cached original. Training could use pairs while
inference still takes one photo. It is neither physical skin-colour truth nor
a demonstrated solution. A small pilot is not sufficient training data or a
fairness study, and its extra-data status must be explicit in the paper.

This is not a new completion prerequisite. Before deciding whether it is practical,
we need only know whether a device can lock exposure and white balance. No photos,
charts, purchases, subjects or capture activities are requested yet. If unavailable
or the user wants existing datasets only, retain that constraint and assess a
specifically different correction-data source within it; do not silently acquire
a dataset, restart old screens or invent an illumination cue.

The accompanying [resource-gated pilot draft](correction-capture-feasibility-draft-2026-11-11.md)
is concrete enough to assess scope but is NOT an instruction to capture or a
training order. The earlier broad capture proposal remains preserved; this draft
removes any implication that charts or measured intrinsic colour are required.
