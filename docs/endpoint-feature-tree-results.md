# Endpoint features improve the source-held correction candidate

30 October 2026. Adding six masked R/G/B endpoint fractions substantially
improves this development comparison: combined CIEDE2000 falls from 8.013 to
5.300 versus the stronger of the two matched 47-pair baselines, and unwanted
clean changes fall from two to one. This is the strongest current candidate
on these exposed development cases. It still fails the zero-clean-change
requirement, so the demo model is unchanged.

The [frozen protocol](endpoint-feature-tree-protocol.md) declared one addition:
for each R/G/B channel, the fraction of masked samples exactly 0 and exactly
255. No endpoint tolerance, bin search, skip threshold or per-image exception.
These are input-only encoded-image statistics, not measured sensor clipping.
The five existing Lab/ITA/experimental-MST features remain. Targets are direct
gamma/red/blue multipliers; depth 3, seed 42, minimum leaf size 1 and fitting-
control protection stay fixed. All 47 admitted rows and their concerns remain.

Candidate fitting holds out all variants of each of 26 source/known duplicate
groups. Two independently verified 47-pair baselines are reused unchanged,
avoiding unnecessary refitting. New candidate outputs are source-held, not
predictions on the candidate's own fitting rows.

Lower CIEDE2000 is better. Historical and new subsets remain separate.

The candidate improves both distorted subsets, rather than lowering the pooled
mean only through an easier added cohort. It changes 13/15 historical distorted
inputs and 5/6 new distorted inputs; changing an image is not itself evidence
of improvement. Its historical 30 error also beats the earlier historical-only
protected model's 7.132, though that earlier model used different fitting data.
Do not present that last comparison as an isolated feature effect.

## Failure and visual limits

The remaining held clean failure is **68453**. Its error is about 12.23 while
the other 25 clean outputs are unchanged. Visual inspection confirms obvious
facial overcorrection and mask-shaped boundaries. No exception for that source
was introduced. The candidate remains a failed safety-gate result despite the
large average gain.

Also inspected 53109, selected explicitly as the largest distorted gain against
the clean-augmented baseline. Correction moves the face toward the reference,

## What remains before adoption

Endpoint statistics may exploit clipping patterns specific to the synthetic
recipe. JPEG encoding can alter exact endpoints, and real photographs without
such patterns may behave differently. The current cohort is small, repeatedly
exposed and not final validation. Weak experimental MST calibration, limited
tone coverage, accepted mask concerns, clipped approximate targets and missing
facial/neck coverage remain. No reserved data or new calibration was opened.

The next justified checks are input-encoding sensitivity and held-clean safety
of this frozen candidate, without retuning to 68453. Preserve the complete
candidate and its failure for those checks. A passing average, portable tree
export or successful unit suite does not authorize calling the thesis complete
or silently replacing the working demo.
