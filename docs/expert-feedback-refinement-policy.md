# Professional feedback and model refinement

31 October2026 — explicit user clarification. This supersedes any interpretation
of earlier notes that would prohibit refinement after professionals first see
the results. It does not start expert evaluation immediately.

The adopted sequence is:

1. Obtain a good initial correction model. The currently rejected candidates
   do not meet that condition; passing software tests alone is insufficient.
2. Ask qualified professionals to review results through the existing Google
   Form and provide ratings and comments.
3. Use relevant feedback to make a documented improvement to the correction
   model, preserving the retained method unless a change is explicitly approved.
4. Ask professionals to review the revised results again. Keep model versions,
   image membership and each round's ratings/comments separate.
5. Once the model is frozen, conduct/report a separate final appraisal under a
   declared evaluation procedure. Earlier feedback rounds remain development
   evidence and cannot be renamed untouched final validation.

This matches the proposal's professional input during testing/refinement. The
previous timing direction meant not to seek professional opinions before a good
initial model, not to ban later refinement or repeat appraisal. The two-expert
proposal remains a proposal; no participation, responses or scores are assumed.
The user has not authorized sending invitations, modifying the Form or contacting
professionals in this turn.

The model versions, issues raised, chosen changes and subsequent results should
be recorded together. Final evaluation membership and stopping conditions must
be declared before final appraisal. No number of rounds, new model family or
guaranteed improvement is invented here. Current training and parser/beard holds
remain in effect while the adviser decision is pending.
