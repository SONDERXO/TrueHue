**Decision: not a ready correction-reference cohort.** Person tags alone do not
establish usable portraits. Two prominent-face cases could justify further source
inspection, but are not a new two-source training cohort or approved clean subset.
All six remain unadmitted. This is one small feasibility result, not an estimate
of the usable fraction of all FiveK or a finding that no suitable portraits exist.
Thumbnail appearance cannot establish full-resolution blur, clipping, accurate
colour, actual selected skin, independent identities, age or MST labels. Do not
transfer thumbnail review to future full-resolution masks or targets.

The promised six-source check is complete. No automatic replacement, larger
sampling, source fit or training follows. A later full-resolution inspection
needs a fixed acquisition limit and exact source list, colour-profile handling
and the same photographic-restoration claim. FiveK still does not supply reliable
matching MST or verified intrinsic skin colour. Retain the existing correction
method, masks, model and reserved data roles.
