## Decision

This shortlist does not provide an admitted facial recoverability roster.
Six images show body/limb/chest views without usable facial geometry. Three show
partial facial regions; none establishes both an accepted photographic reference
and current measurement/application support. Do not spend target-fit runs on
this roster or automatically expand the SCIN inventory.

The darker-skin priority candidates were inspected first. Of the three labelled
dark by either annotator, two are nonfacial views and one is a very dim,
warm-lit partial face with its eyes redacted. This decision concerns framing,
reference suitability and pipeline support, not skin darkness itself. Dark skin
must not be rejected merely because it is dark. Both annotations remain intact;
no clinical diagnosis, skin-tone relabeling or physical-colour judgment was made.
