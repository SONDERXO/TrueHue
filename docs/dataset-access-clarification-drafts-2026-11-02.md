Subject: STW release and label-provenance clarification for an MST thesis

Dear STW team,

We are assessing STW as a potential source of MST labels for a skin-tone measurement
and correction thesis. We have inspected the repository and public annotation CSV,
without downloading the image collection or running the repository code.

Could you clarify the intended current release and associated permitted uses?
The downloaded all_annotated_data.csv has 42,312 unique image paths and 3,587 distinct
new_tokens keys, while the paper reports 42,313 images and 3,564 people. Is there a
corrected release, identity crosswalk or recommended annotation/split revision?

We would also like to understand:

- Label provenance, available independent-rater labels/disagreement information
  and whether any labels or subject groupings remain provisional.
- The relationship between identity keys across source datasets and any known
  duplicate identities/images or excluded records.
- Source-specific terms permitting label use, calibration/model development,
  evaluation and thesis example reproduction; especially components requiring
  separate provider access.
- Mapping back to exact FairFace source IDs, so existing development/reserved
  allocations can be respected without creating overlap or leakage.

The intended method retains ITA with calibrated MST and a correction decision
tree; We are not assuming that STW supplies instrument-verified colour targets or
that its associated classifier must replace that method.

Thank you for your guidance.

Kind regards,

Nythan John G. Rivera
Oceane Rim Dardare
University of San Carlos
