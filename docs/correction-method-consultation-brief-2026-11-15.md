# TrueHue: focused correction-method consultation

Prepared 15 November 2026. No contact sent or image attachments authorized.

**Decision needed:** Identify one defensible feature-to-control learning intervention for automatic exposure AND colour correction that transfers between people while preserving acceptable complexion. Specify its inference-available information, assumptions and smallest falsifiable test. Another algorithm name alone is insufficient.

**Paper requirement:** Single-photo ITA/MST and image statistics feed a decision tree predicting gamma/red/blue controls. Synthetic distorted photographs are restored toward their original photographic references. The proposal explicitly does not claim physical skin-colour recovery or definitive real-camera-bias correction (PDF14/63). Approved research extensions include frozen features, different final predictor assessments, experimental controls and moderate camera-inspired conditions; none is a final adopted method. Alternate pretrained pixel-level correction networks remain excluded. Friday Nov20 before noon Manila is the working deadline, with no completion assurance.

**Evidence:** Eleven already-exposed FFHQ reference sources lack official tone labels. Earlier ordinary trees failed colour transfer. One frozen-feature nearest-neighbour candidate had 11identity cases accepted and10/11colour results useful, but the user rejected its exposure appearance. Gentler half-stop camera-inspired inputs with known inverse supervision still produced colour RGB error12.050 versus input6.564; paired nuisance projection/prototypes gave11.523. Their implementations passed independent verification; neither candidate was adopted.

**Latest bounded investigation:** A frozen external rendered-WB model provided33mapping coefficients as features without applying any external portrait correction. All66inputs were processed. On fixed synthetic probe responses, mean paired cast direction was positive at all3exposures, but the predeclared balanced absolute-cue error failed:0.0944 versus zero-cue0.0407. This is NOT image reconstruction error or a trained314feature-tree failure; no such tree was fitted. This stop does not prove the coefficient representation unusable for all learned predictors. It does not justify automatically overriding the gate or starting a new predictor search.

**Separate unfinished evidence:** Independent tone-qualified evaluation and a validated development MST map remain unresolved. These limit subgroup/bias claims; they are not a requirement for intrinsic colour measurements or labels on every exploratory training portrait. Current research model Android parity and actual photographer MOS are incomplete. ECPD access requested by the user, but editor pairs are optional supplementary evidence, not a proposal prerequisite or guaranteed learning repair.

**Questions for review:**

1. Is there a credible input-available cue or prior that can distinguish an imposed cast from acceptable complexion under this synthetic photographic task, given the source-transfer failures?
2. Does the latest coefficient proxy test the right prerequisite for feature usefulness? If not, explain the mismatch and propose a prospectively fixed test with genuine new justification, preserving the completed failure rather than tuning its criterion.
3. What single supervision/representation change would address the mechanism, rather than increase fitting capacity on the same11people? What observation should stop it?
4. Which aspects of the written three-control/decision-tree method would need amendment, and what attainable data evidence would the proposed intervention actually require?

No request to solve this by inferring race as tone, imposing a common complexion, opening locked data, repeating closed source hunts or ignoring unwanted changes. All original variants must remain source-grouped. No additional portraits are available from the user. Known severe clipping cannot be credited with restored detail merely from lower mean error.
