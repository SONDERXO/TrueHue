Subject: Research access request — Skin Tone Estimation under Diverse Lighting Conditions dataset

Dear Dr Theart,

We are Nythan John G. Rivera and Oceane Rim Dardare from the University of San
Carlos. We are developing TrueHue, a thesis project on photographic facial
skin-tone correction using ITA, calibrated Monk Skin Tone and a decision-tree
model with gamma, red-gain and blue-gain outputs.

We found your paper, “Skin Tone Estimation under Diverse Lighting Conditions,”
and its data-availability statement. Could we request access to the labelled
facial-image dataset for noncommercial academic research?

Our immediate purpose is to investigate the relationship between facial ITA
measurements and supplied MST labels across lighting conditions, using held-out
subjects. We are not conducting facial recognition or claiming recovery of
someone's intrinsic skin colour.

Could you please advise on:

- Research access terms, any fees, and whether fitting an ITA-to-MST calibration
  and reporting aggregate results or derived model parameters is permitted.
- Availability of original images, anonymous subject IDs, MST labels and their
  labelling/consensus procedure, plus lighting and pose metadata.
- The number of different participants per MST category and any known label,
  colour-processing or capture limitations we should preserve in our analysis.
- Whether a small labelled inspection sample can be provided first, and any
  restrictions on storing data or showing example images in a thesis.

We would assess feasibility before committing to larger processing and follow
the applicable research-use and attribution conditions. Please let us know what
institutional information or documentation you would need for the request.

Thank you for your time.

Nythan John G. Rivera and Oceane Rim Dardare
University of San Carlos
