## Literature findings screened before implementation

**Maximum-entropy inverse gamma (AGT-ME/GCME).** The accessible [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.02246) gives a lightweight input-only calculation and ROI support. However:

- Equations8–11 maximize transformed-image entropy. That objective does not explicitly preserve the selected original photographic rendering.
- SectionIV-B obtains relative added-gamma accuracy by comparing estimates on original and distorted images; the reported error is not proof of recovering TrueHue's added edit from the distorted image alone.
- The paper's property3 maps a constant intensity to `exp(-1)`. As an analytical illustration, an acceptable constant patch at0.2 would change to approximately0.368. This is not a real-portrait failure measurement, but rules out treating the formula as inherently preservation-safe.
- Direct application would replace the learned decision, while using its output as a new feature still requires unestablished transfer/preservation evidence. Neither is selected as a ready repair.

**Frequency-domain blind inverse gamma.** [Farid's primary abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18255487/) describes minimizing higher-order frequency correlations. The author's full-PDF fetch timed out; no claim of reading/reproducing its complete equations or verified implementation. This is a distinct literature mechanism, but no ready, qualified added-edit feature for the retained pipeline was established. Total-response estimation must not be equated with the correction relative to an unknown acceptable processed original. The existing edge/response ambiguity result remains relevant and closed; this does not prove every frequency feature impossible.

The completed histogram-gap studies remain closed. No new sampling/threshold/feature rescue follows from its reappearance in search.
