The next redesign review must separate two possible interventions rather than

## Decision from existing evidence

Do not promote an existing candidate or restart the failed leaf/feature cycle.
Keep the working automatic model as the objective; parser training is deferred.
The completion specification records the unchanged gates and the NO-GO result.

The evidence supports two required capabilities, not a proven new architecture:

- Distinguish genuinely overexposed inputs needing correction from clean inputs
  currently sharing identity leaves. Fifteen documented non-veto misses share
  leaves with 79–82 clean fitting rows; identity is also the lowest unconstrained
  aggregate-cost action. Merely removing the clean constraint is not a repair.
- Distinguish colour-cast inputs from underexposure requiring strong brightening.
  A known stress input was brightened from RGB error 6.67 to 31.53. Local leaf
  repair reproduced the harm, while broader nonharm protection lost recovery.

These are exposed-data descriptions, not proofs of a causal mechanism. They do
not show that beard contamination caused the wrong actions or that more tree
depth, synthetic variants or a two-stage classifier would fix them.

## Substantive redesign direction for feasibility review

Investigate whether the automatic input contains additional, repeatable evidence
of correction need, separate from absolute skin brightness and estimated MST.
ITA/MST must retain a documented role; naturally darker complexion must not be
treated as sufficient evidence of underexposure. No new representation is
selected here: the previous green statistics and face-local representation
already failed to resolve the problem. Renaming those interventions or adding
a classifier on the same statistics is not new information.

A training-only feasibility brief must make the redesign concrete before a new
comparison is proposed. It must identify:

1. Exactly one input representation or data-coverage intervention, with an
   operational definition and availability on one ordinary photograph. No
   clean-reference differences, paired target masks or unavailable metadata at
   inference. State on-device compute and asset implications.
2. Evidence from eligible fitting/reserved sources that this supplies information
   missing from the failed variants. Distinguish descriptive overlap from proof
   of non-identifiability; do not claim the task impossible from these traces.
3. A fixed candidate construction, roles and grouping, unchanged ITA/MST and
   masking contracts unless explicitly part of the one intervention, and exact
   fit/compute bounds. Do not move exposed evaluation cases into fitting.
4. Both failure types addressed together, plus clean, underexposure, colour-only
   and combined conditions. A dedicated fix for one named evaluation case is
   ineligible. Do not optimise abstention thresholds on the exposed outcomes.
5. Unchanged acceptance and independent verification, exhaustive changed-output
   review, and a declared single-comparison stop. No automatic fallback fit or
   parameter sweep after failure.

If no credible single-image evidence or eligible fitting coverage is identified,
stop at that finding. Do not start training to create an appearance of progress.
Then a concrete decision is needed about acquiring genuinely different evidence,
changing the product requirements, or reporting the research limitation. None
is silently approved here. No new manual mask step or new input capture is
substituted for the user's automatic single-photo workflow.
