11 November 2026. Scripts 832-833; saved development JSON/text only. No photo, archive, final24 or older locked-image bytes read; no fits, new predictions, acquisition, runtime edits or annotations. PPR access is paused as an optional extension, not a main-study dependency.

## What was established

- All 44 saved float32 feature vectors are distinct. Independent exhaustive comparison of all 946 pairs finds no identical-vector conflicting-target case. This rules out that narrow explanation on these records, not ambiguity in real photographs or failure of generalization.
- All 11 original/cool pairs differ in measured features, including facial L/a/b/ITA and scene red/green ratio. Yet 10 pairs route to the same leaf in the saved bounded-search tree. The four green features remain identical within each such pair, as previously documented; other features do carry a change. The problem is not total disappearance of the red-cast signal.
- For 16061, 48879 and 46795, all four held roles have four distinct vectors and four distinct target-control vectors but reach one leaf. This is observed misrouting of distinguishable inputs, not identical-input impossibility. It does not justify deeper trees or another search budget: the completed comparison already tested its fixed class and remains closed.
- Every one of the 16 scalar features has overlapping observed intervals for every pair of roles (96 overlaps). Thus none of these observed role ranges is wholly separated by one scalar interval. Marginal overlap does NOT prove multivariate inseparability, a required classifier, or information-theoretic impossibility. No distances, thresholds or classifiers were selected from this descriptive finding.
- Every source has the same four target triples: identity [1,1,1], under [.7,1,1], over [1/.7,1,1], cool [1,255/245,1]. There are three gamma targets, two red targets and one blue target. Source IDs or recipe names are training metadata, never inference inputs. Equal inverse controls for an equal known transform are correct; do not force tone-specific outputs merely to make the model appear tone-aware.
- All 11 original references receive the same MST estimate, 4.4947368421. Only the underexposed variants of 29617 and 46795 depart from it, to approximately 7 and 6.65594. There are zero provided MST labels. This is variation induced by input appearance, not independent evidence of underlying tone coverage or successful MST conditioning. ITA varies, but its independent correction value has not been established by these results. A new calibrator or MST sweep is not selected.

## Go/no-go and the decision now needed

NO-GO for another fit under the current tested setup. No distinct supported correction repair has emerged from this assessment. Unique vectors do not imply that memorization or a larger tree will transfer; scalar overlaps do not imply a different model family is necessary. Preserve the final decision tree, ITA/MST and single-photo interface.

The defensible next research direction, if selected, is a substantive review of the input representation: seek a specifically justified single-photo cue that could distinguish correction need from natural complexion/content across sources, using existing datasets only. This would be a declared method change, not another threshold/descriptor grid. Before any extractor or fit, it must specify available-at-inference information, why existing summaries lose relevant information, resource and licence implications, source-group evaluation and a stop rule. No particular cue or extractor is validated or chosen here; no success is promised. ITA/MST stay in the input contract and independent tone evidence still needs honest treatment.

The user now needs to choose whether to allow that substantive input-design departure from the present paper feature implementation, or require strict retention of the current feature design. Under strict retention, there is no currently evidence-backed next training run to recommend. Do not substitute a negative-results thesis for the working-model goal, impose adviser approval, or disguise PPR retrieval as the solution. Stop technical experimentation at this decision; do not initiate a generic literature/source hunt before scope is settled.
