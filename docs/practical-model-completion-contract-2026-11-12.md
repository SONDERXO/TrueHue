## What successful future correction must demonstrate

- Useful improvement toward the declared photographic reference relative to uncorrected input. Report exposure directions and colour conditions separately so aggregate exposure gains cannot hide harmful colour treatment. Returning every input unchanged does not meet the useful-correction requirement.
- Conservative behaviour: missed corrections are reported, not silently removed. Individual harms, worst failures, unchanged decisions, attempted coverage and unavailable results remain visible. The preference does not authorize a new confidence gate, identity veto, prediction clipping, correction-strength schedule or changed controls.
- Preservation of already acceptable references under a predefined visual assessment. Tiny numeric differences are not automatically failures, but an average error or universal DeltaE number does not establish that a change is visually unnoticeable. Visible unwanted brightening, darkening or colour shift does not satisfy the selected policy. No perceptual threshold is inferred from historical outcomes.
- Exact preservation of user-protected and outside-alpha pixels relative to actual input. This is distinct from the selected identity-image visual tolerance.
- One compatible measurement/MST/features/tree/rendering contract, source-group separation, fitting-only calibration/choices and honest tonal coverage. Retaining an MST column alone is not evidence of tone-aware benefit. No fake labels or fairness claims.
- Prospective evidence beyond fitting and repeatedly exposed development, with the qualifying method frozen before its corresponding independent evaluation. Final24 and older locked image bytes remain unread until a specifically defined later evaluation stage.

The model learns photographic restoration, not calibrated intrinsic skin colour. Perfect masks, exactly invariant MST and a new physical-colour dataset are not invented universal prerequisites. Real support and calibration limitations must still be disclosed.
