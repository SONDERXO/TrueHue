capacity benefit under these conditions. This test asks whether that benefit
survives prediction. It is not another strength-only rescue of the closed
gamma/gain-blue recipe and is not a new claim of a colour-disambiguating cue.

This is a deliberately high-risk capacity-to-prediction feasibility test.
An exposure gain cannot rescue a colour or identity failure. It cannot establish
improved performance on the old mild review cases, physical skin-colour truth,
