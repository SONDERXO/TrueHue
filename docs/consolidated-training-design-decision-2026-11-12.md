## The precise evidence gap

We have not established an inference-available relationship between the retained lightweight measurements and the appropriate correction direction that transfers across source content. Distinct full feature vectors and good known-control reconstruction show that this is not a proved identical-input contradiction or a necessary renderer failure on the simple recipes. They do not identify a learnable robust rule. Reaching zero fitting loss for a fold does not supply held-source evidence. This is a gap in demonstrated prediction, not proof that a second photo, physical measurement, perfect masks or every possible new dataset is required.

A genuinely new data design would have to name what differently qualified observations it adds, why those observations teach that relationship rather than merely increase repeated recipes, an inference contract consistent with singlephoto/traditional features, source-group and mapping roles, and one prospective correction test/stop. No currently reviewed, ready existing-data design meets that burden. These are design questions, not new numeric runtime boundaries.
