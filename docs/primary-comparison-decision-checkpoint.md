## What changed in our understanding

Recoverable normal-exposure training helps normal exposure reconstruction.
It does not resolve unwanted clean correction and reduces clipped-stress
performance. Therefore “the model only needs unclipped training examples” is
not a sufficient explanation or a completed solution. The user's reviews and
new data remain useful evidence; the failed fit is not a reason to relabel them.

The prepared data increase variants, not independent source diversity: there
are still 26 original source groups. Controls mean “leave this dataset reference
unchanged”, not independently calibrated true skin color or experimentally
verified correct lighting. Mask acceptance validates the chosen mask scope,
not that photometric-reference assumption.

ITA and the current MST estimate are derived from the same facial Lab values.
They do not independently tell the model whether a naturally darker face is
underexposed. That is a plausible cause of the observed confusion, not proof
that the proposed feature family can never work. Prior audits found no exact
cross-source feature collisions. The small, exposed sample and weak experimental
MST calibration do not support an impossibility or final-generalization claim.
