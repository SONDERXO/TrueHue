1. **Training eligibility and support:** the technical records do not establish
   dense skin quality. Preserve all eight outcomes and define any future eligibility
   prospectively; do not quietly fit the seven successes or discard1188_0.
2. **MST/feature compatibility:** the current experimental green model declares
   16features including `input_mst_score`, with measurement contract
   `c750c96d2465675a94f6595bc2dd14adacd5d6b6c8841ff08448e43dbeffe752`.
   The new native-anchor preparation has not qualified matching MST/calibration
   for these controls. Four measured Lab/ITA values alone do not complete that
   schema. Do not invent PPR MST labels, substitute known source tone at inference,
   round scores arbitrarily or copy an old lookup by matching column names.
3. **Grouped fitting and validation:** photographic pairs and controls must have
   declared roles, training costs and source-group exclusions. These eight exposed
   groups cannot act as fresh independent final evidence. Keep the original
   development comparators and quality gates; do not manufacture a favourable
   new denominator to pass them.
4. **Single substantive intervention:** if a learning design becomes justified,
   change supervision only, keeping the tree/input/operator settings fixed under
   a matched contract. Do not bundle richer features, altered weights/depth or a
   new gate with it. A concrete fixed comparison and failure disposition are
   required before fitting. No fit is selected by this readiness result.
