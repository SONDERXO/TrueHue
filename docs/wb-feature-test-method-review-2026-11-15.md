The external WB candidate originally proposed using33mapping coefficients as INPUT features. The completed six-probe proxy tested the native mapping's absolute chromatic response against a desired inverse cast and neutral response. That is an interpretable diagnostic, but not a necessary condition for downstream feature usefulness. It cannot establish that a learned314feature model failed; no such model was fitted. I was too quick to frame researcher consultation as the only useful next step after that proxy stop.

Analytical counterexample (not TrueHue evidence): a scalar feature z=10+y, y in{0,1}, has a large error when z is treated directly as a correction estimate. A stump with threshold10.5 and leaf values0/1 recovers y exactly. Thus bad absolute feature-as-output error does not logically imply useless input information. This example supplies no assurance that natural source-dependent WB offsets are removable: those offsets may overlap the edit signal on unseen people.

The proxy remains FAILED/CLOSED under its predeclared criterion. Do not change thresholds, subtract offsets, rescore it or claim features pass from mean directional response. Positive paired directions are descriptive evidence; they do not establish held-person inference because clean pairs are unavailable at inference. The clinical/photographic/fairness constraints remain unchanged.

## Concrete direct-test proposal

Question: on the already authorized moderate camera-inspired development task, does adding frozen, externally correction-supervised coefficients improve actual source-held control prediction and rendered image quality relative to an otherwise identical traditional-feature tree?

The additional information basis is the external WB-error supervision, not another tree depth, loss weighting, source selection or generic ReIQA representation. The completed coefficients show a response to imposed casts, with substantial natural-source offsets and individual misses retained. Those observations justify reviewing a direct test; they do not establish its success. Source-transfer failure remains the central falsifiable risk.

Proposed fixed comparison, before any training:

- Same66existing camera-task cases,11source-held folds,60fit/6held, no additions or removals. This is reused exposed development, not independent final evaluation or labelled-tone qualification.
- Two declared arms: all281traditional features including ITA/MST versus those same281plus all33raw WBcoefficients. No ReIQA, coefficient selection, centering/calibration/weight/feature search, test-time clean reference or external corrected pixels.
- One ordinary multioutput CART recipe for both arms: depth4, seed42, otherwise explicitly frozen identical settings. At most22fold fits total, not22alternative algorithms. No hyperparameter tuning or second recipe after results.
- Same approved camera analytic targets and existing experimental luminance/chroma renderer, masks/alpha/protection/manual ears, so representation is the changed factor. This does NOT restore literal proposal gamma/R/B output semantics: retaining camera controls is an already-authorized research extension. A separate final method decision remains necessary; no hidden switch to a third renderer or paper-parity claim.
- Known inverse controls remain supervision-only; no target optimizer, mapper/encoder/baseline target refit. All fitted models independently verified before any held prediction, all132arm-specific held controls sealed before reference scoring. Share one independently verified fit-only constant comparator per fold, not a new optimization.
- Evaluate every exposure/colour role using unchanged primary RGB and CIEDE2000 definitions versus input and fit-only constant, with all harms and identity changes retained. Adding WBfeatures must improve colour relative to the matched281arm; superiority of one arm to the other is insufficient if it fails the original correction-benefit requirements. No pooling away exposure failures. Detailed strict comparisons/tolerances and handling of equal results must be frozen in an executable protocol before training.
- Any numerical advance requires inspection and new candidate-specific human usefulness/preservation review. Old ratings cannot transfer. No strict zero identity requirement is newly imposed; acceptable slight changes remain permitted.

This is a proposal, NOT an executable fitting contract: code bindings, exact model settings, export/schema verifier, source-safe loaders, generated rehearsal, all-feature/row joins and full scoring/stop rules still need specification. No fit is authorized merely by this document. A failed comparison must remain closed without a model/source/preprocessing rescue queue. No final all-data fit or Android integration is scheduled.

## What can be finished and what cannot be promised

We have neither proved the retained method impossible nor demonstrated an adequate full correction model. A further justified direct test is possible in principle; a successful outcome is uncertain. Completion requires actual automatic exposure AND colour usefulness, preserved complexion, a frozen selected model, supported evaluation and then compatible device integration. Small exposed development cannot establish independent darker-skin benefit; the proposed test supplies no new tone labels, validated mapper, expert MOS or Android qualification.
