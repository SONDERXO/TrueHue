## Evidence availability

Tracked protocols/results and code describe the research; the selected exact scalar snapshot provides readable aggregates. Full local `outputs/` archives, photographs, masks, model binaries and raw reviews are not published in Git. They remain separate provenance evidence. The writing assistant must distinguish a result reported in a dated record from independently opening its underlying data.

Public study records may include source keys and aggregate development ratings. Personal appeal-letter material and its generated scripts are excluded from this update. They are not model-development evidence needed for thesis claims. Existing historical draft files remain labelled drafts, even when their filenames contain "thesis" or "conference".
