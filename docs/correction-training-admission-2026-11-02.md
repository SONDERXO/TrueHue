## What would actually advance the correction model

The next useful research work is **a measurement-admission protocol for a bounded
expanded MST development cohort**, before any new pixel processing. It must
specify exact labelled subjects/images and exposure/duplicate roles, what evidence
qualifies actual selected skin, who supplies that evidence, handling of uncertainty
and coverings, and a manageable assessment workload. Assistant inspection alone
does not establish mask accuracy. Representative approval cannot silently approve
every other photograph of a person. No new review batch is created here.

That protocol must also justify the restricted development question and task
adequacy criteria before outcomes. Preserve the fixed FaceOcc implementation;
keep all attempted failures/holds in reporting; no selection on mapping errors.
If it cannot produce a defensible eligible cohort, stop before fitting. Another
metadata recount, software replay or small old-subject fit cannot resolve this.

Only an admitted cohort can activate one matching, subject-held MST evaluation.
Only sufficiently justified mapping results can activate a separate correction
training protocol with broader admitted photographic sources, current blended
targets, grouped evaluation and unchanged-image controls. A successful synthetic
reconstruction result supports photograph restoration, not recovered true colour.
No automatic lookup export, five-source fit, runtime switch or final-validation
claim follows from this check. Reserved correction roles remain locked.

This preserves correction-first priority. Phone testing and additional delivery
packaging do not solve these research prerequisites. No participant recruitment,
purchase, adviser wait or professional contact is requested. Existing Google Form
review remains after a good initial correction model.
