# Six-case skin-patch reference protocol — 6 November 2026

The user explicitly agrees to prepare the short manual patch review. Reuse
exactly the six sources from the completed point review, including the landmark
failure. Do not repeat point labels, prefill polygons, display scores/predictions
or request dense whole-face annotation. Preserve originals and previous exports.

Show the native original face crop and full original scene, nearest-neighbour
zoom only. Reviewer may outline one or more confident facial-skin patches or
record no reliable patch with a reason. Exclude neck, hair/beard/stubble,
features, glasses frames/reflections and uncertain edges. Do not impose a patch
size/count or require matching locations. Native integer vertices, source hashes
and reviewer code bind the export. Unmarked regions remain unknown.

Patch boundaries are reviewer-selected development references, not dense skin
truth, photographic colour calibration or independent validation. Regional
illumination, tiny native crops and reviewer uncertainty remain. Prior exposure
means the review is not blind. No threshold/mask tuning from these patches.

After the actual export, strictly validate exact membership, image and manifest
hashes, geometry, completion/status and reviewer identity before rasterizing.
Keep original export bytes. Compare automatic masks against these patches and
original-pixel Lab/ITA statistics under a separately recorded manual-reference
contract. Retain unavailable outcomes, including references unavailable despite
an automatic technical pass, and references available despite detector failure.
No invented minimum-native-pixel adequacy rule or automatic1000pixel guard
transfer. Polygon unions are sampled patch support, not whole-face recall.

Preparing the page performs no import, measurement, inference, training,
admission or app change. Stop for actual user responses. The dataset, review
and labels remain private under author research/noncommercial, citation and
no-redistribution terms. Do not upload screenshots or reviews publicly.
