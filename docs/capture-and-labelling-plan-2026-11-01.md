## 2. Two needs, with separate evidence

**Correction references:** clear, consistently lit portraits with documented
settings and repeat captures. These help define a photographic target.

**MST labels:** independent, traceable category evidence for identifiable subject
groups. Do not label from TrueHue ITA, its failed mapping, race or whichever
label produces better results. Capture lighting does not itself establish MST.
Before gathering labels, specify the reference scale, annotation conditions,
annotators, uncertainty/disagreement handling and provenance. No annotator
agreement procedure is invented or described as validated here.

Official MST-E has only one subject each at7/10. At least one additional
independent person in each would be needed just to make same-category
calibration/evaluation separation possible; that is a structural floor, not an
adequate sample-size recommendation. Full-dataset counts do not imply available
approved masks. Categories4/5/9 also need additional eligible support relative to
the completed15-subject cycle. The four outside-cycle subjects are historically
exposed, not untouched. Neither FairFace estimated tones nor SCIN disagreement
filtering fixes these gaps. Freeze actual subject counts and scope before capture.

## 3. One bounded acquisition-feasibility stage

First establish available phone/camera, app controls, stable diffuse lighting,
target/card availability, willing participants and permitted thesis use. No
equipment purchase is assumed. If controls cannot be locked or independent
lighting evidence is unavailable, record the reduced claim rather than promise
true-colour recovery. Avoid unverified household objects as calibrated targets.

Prepare one short feasibility session before any broader collection. Its
participant count, exact captures, data roles and repeatability criteria must be
filled in and frozen once resources are known. Until then this is not an
executable capture order. Use existing equipment where suitable; do not start
recruiting or contacting anyone through this agent.

For each planned session:

1. Assign coded person/session IDs and roles before images are measured. Record
   consent/use scope, known duplicate relationships and previous exposure.
2. Record device/app/lens, lighting arrangement and camera settings. Keep pose,
   distance and lighting consistent. Record unsupported exposure/white-balance
   locks and automatic processing; preserve original files.
3. If a suitable documented target is available, photograph it under matching
   lighting/settings, then take the target-free portrait and repeat reference
   frames. Log changes; target frames stay outside runtime inputs.
4. Check focus, casts, uneven light, clipping and repeat consistency separately
   from masks. Record makeup, hair, glasses and coverings. Keep every attempt
   and hold reason, including failed measurement. A beard alone is not exclusion;
   selected beard pixels are a measurement problem.
5. Report the fixed session once and stop. If reference evidence or repeatability
   is inadequate, do not train, refill the session or tune mask/tree settings.

No deliberately changed-light captures or synthetic pairs are required during
feasibility. They need a later frozen design; exposure settings are not known
inverse gamma targets and differently posed captures are not aligned pixel truth.
