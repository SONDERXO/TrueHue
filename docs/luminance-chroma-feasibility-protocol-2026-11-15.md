## Frozen formula

Decode normalizedsRGB to linearRGBu using standard piecewise2.4transfer. Parameters in order gamma q, exposure gain g, red balance r, blue balance b, chroma scale s. Let v=u*[r,1,b], Y=dot(v,[.2126,.7152,.0722]). For Y=0 outputlinearblack exactly; no darkness threshold or epsilon. Otherwise L=clip(g*Y**q,0,1), delta=L*s*(v/Y-1). Choose largest t in[0,1] for which every component of L+t*delta lies in[0,1], by the exact channelwise ray bounds. Outputlinear=L+t*delta. This preserves desired linear luminance up to floating error and the direction of the linearRGBchroma ray; NOT perceptual-hue preservation. Encode sRGB; apply existing alpha in encodedRGB after transform, force existing protected/outside pixels unchanged, round once toRGB8. Never edit/dilate masks.

Identity[1,1,1,1,1]. Bounds q/g/r/b each[.25,4], s[.25,1.5]; no full-gray s0, no mask/skin-neutral template or tone-label inference. Bounds frozen before generated/real outcomes; no expansion/retries. Positive arbitrarily dark pixels participate, protecting darker-skin intent from black-threshold exclusions.
